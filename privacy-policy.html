Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies when you visit this website, send an enquiry, ask about advertising or collaboration, communicate with us by email, or engage with ORBIT in relation to potential or active SEO consulting services. It also applies to business contact information processed during normal professional relationship management.

The controller responsible for the processing described here is ORBIT SEO CONSULTING, located at WARSAW, POLAND. Privacy requests may be sent to HELLO@ORBIT-SEO.COM.

This policy concerns personal data relating to identifiable individuals. It does not apply to information that has been irreversibly anonymised so that no person can reasonably be identified from it.

Personal data we collect

We collect only the information reasonably required to answer an enquiry, evaluate a potential engagement, deliver agreed services, operate the website and protect our operations and legal rights.

Identity and contact data
Your name, business email address, company name, professional role, website address and any contact details you choose to provide.
Enquiry and communication data
The subject of your request, selected area of interest, objectives, budget or timing information, correspondence, meeting notes and other information included in messages or documents you send.
Client and project data
Contract details, project contacts, instructions, approvals, deliverables, account or website access information supplied for an engagement, invoices and payment administration records.
Website and device data
IP address, browser and device type, operating system, requested URL, referring page, date and time, response status and security-related event information that may appear in hosting or server logs.
Public professional data
Business contact information, professional profile information and company information available from corporate websites, professional platforms, public registers or referrals where relevant to a legitimate business conversation.

We do not ask for special-category data such as health, biometric, political, religious or trade-union information. Please do not include sensitive personal data, passwords, payment-card data or confidential credentials in the website form.

Where personal data comes from

Most information is provided directly by you when you complete the contact form, write to us, join a call, exchange project materials or enter into an agreement. If you contact us on behalf of a company, that company may also provide your business contact information.

We may receive professional contact data from a colleague, referral partner or publicly available business source when there is a reasonable basis to believe that the subject is relevant to our services. We may also generate project records and professional observations while performing consulting work.

Technical data is generated automatically by the systems used to deliver and secure the website. The site does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels or behavioural profiling; it stores one local consent record as described in the Cookies Policy.

Website and forms

Contact forms

When you submit a form, the information is transmitted to the website server and delivered to the corporate email address configured for this site. Form data is used to review the request and reply. A hidden anti-spam field is used to identify automated submissions. The form does not create a user account or subscribe you to marketing by default.

Server and security logs

The hosting environment may record standard request and error logs. These records help serve pages, diagnose failures, identify abuse and preserve system security. They are not used by ORBIT to build advertising profiles.

Cookies

The current application does not deploy analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one local consent record to remember the visitor's choice, while hosting or security infrastructure may use strictly necessary mechanisms depending on deployment configuration. See the Cookies Policy for the current inventory and available controls.

Who may receive personal data

We do not sell personal data. Access is limited to people and providers who need the information for a defined business purpose.

  • Website hosting, infrastructure, security, backup and content-delivery providers that support the operation of the site.
  • Email, calendar, document, project-management and collaboration providers used to communicate and deliver work.
  • Independent contractors or specialist partners involved in an engagement, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Accounting, legal, insurance and other professional advisers where access is necessary for their services.
  • Public authorities, regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.
  • A purchaser, investor or successor in connection with a genuine business reorganisation, subject to confidentiality and applicable data-protection requirements.

Processors acting on our instructions may use personal data only for the agreed service and must protect it through contractual and technical safeguards. Independent controllers are responsible for their own processing under their published terms.

International data transfers

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, we use safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. You may contact us for information about the safeguard relevant to a specific transfer.

How long we keep data

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements. The actual period depends on the record and relationship.

General enquiries
Normally up to 24 months after the last meaningful communication, unless the enquiry becomes an active engagement or a longer period is justified by a dispute or legal requirement.
Client and project records
For the duration of the engagement and afterwards for the period required to manage warranties, professional obligations, limitation periods and potential legal claims.
Invoices and tax records
For the period required by applicable accounting and tax law.
Security and server logs
Usually for a short operational period, commonly between 30 and 90 days, unless an event requires longer investigation or the hosting provider applies a different documented retention period.
Marketing preferences
Until consent is withdrawn or an objection is made; a minimal suppression record may be kept to ensure the preference continues to be respected.

At the end of the applicable period, data is deleted, anonymised or securely archived where continued retention is legally required.

Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data and correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion or restriction of processing where applicable;
  • receive and transfer data you provided where portability applies;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests and always object to direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing;
  • not be subject to qualifying decisions based solely on automated processing;
  • lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

To exercise a right, email HELLO@ORBIT-SEO.COM. We may request proportionate information to confirm identity and prevent unauthorised disclosure. We normally respond without undue delay and within one month, subject to any lawful extension for complex or multiple requests.

If you are in Poland, you may lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych). Information is available on the UODO website. You may also contact the supervisory authority in the EU or EEA country of your habitual residence, place of work or alleged infringement.

Security and confidentiality

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or destruction.

No online system is completely secure. Do not send passwords, private keys or highly sensitive material through the general enquiry form. We assess personal-data breaches and make legally required notifications.

Automated decisions and profiling

ORBIT does not use information submitted through this website to make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. We do not create behavioural advertising profiles from website visits.

SEO consulting may involve aggregated analysis of search demand, website performance or content, but that professional analysis is directed at websites and business strategy rather than automated decisions about individual visitors.

Children’s data

This website and the consulting services are intended for businesses and adult professional users. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the enquiry form.

If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate authorisation, contact us so the information can be reviewed and deleted where required.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when relevant practices or obligations change. A revised version will be published here with a new effective date.

Questions about this policy, the use of personal data or a rights request should be sent to HELLO@ORBIT-SEO.COM. Please write “Privacy request” in the subject line and describe the request clearly.
