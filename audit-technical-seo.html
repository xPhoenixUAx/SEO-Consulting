SEE WHAT WE EXAMINE

WHEN THIS WORK MATTERS

A technical audit is a sensible starting point when traffic has dropped, a migration is coming or the site is underperforming and nobody is quite sure why.

  • Unexplained visibility loss
  • Redesign or platform migration
  • Large or complex inventories
  • Growth that has plateaued

MAKE THE SITE EASY TO CRAWL AND UNDERSTAND.

Crawl tools report plenty of imperfections. Our job is to separate the harmless ones from problems that stop important pages being found, indexed or understood correctly.

You receive a ranked plan that explains which pages are affected, why the issue matters, who is best placed to fix it and how we will check the result.

THE SCOPE

WHAT WE CHECK — AND WHAT YOU GET BACK.

SEARCH ACCESS

WHAT WE INSPECT

Robots directives, XML sitemaps, status codes, canonical signals, duplicate URLs, index coverage and the routes search engines use to reach important pages.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

An evidence-backed issue map showing affected templates and URLs, the likely visibility cost and implementation notes that a developer can act on.

PAGE PURPOSE

WHAT WE INSPECT

Titles, headings, metadata, structured data, rendering, template rules and duplicated elements that blur the purpose of priority pages.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Recommendations for each template, worked examples for priority pages and straightforward rules your team can reuse across the site.

SITE STRUCTURE

WHAT WE INSPECT

Click depth, orphan pages, anchor context, faceted navigation and the pathways through which authority moves across the site.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A practical internal-linking map, hub recommendations and repeatable rules that reinforce the pages most important to the business.

QUALITY CONTROL

WHAT WE INSPECT

Mobile rendering, JavaScript dependencies, page experience, deployment risks and whether completed fixes behave as intended in the live environment.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Prioritized development tickets with acceptance criteria, followed by post-release checks so recommendations become verified changes rather than forgotten documents.

HOW WE WORK

FIND THE CAUSE. FIX IT. CHECK IT.

  1. DISCOVER

    See what search engines can reach.

    We combine crawl data, Search Console and a conversation about the business so the audit reflects the site that people actually use.

  2. DIAGNOSE

    Trace problems to their source.

    We look for patterns across templates and page types instead of writing a separate ticket for every affected URL.

  3. PRIORITIZE

    Choose what to fix first.

    Recommendations are ranked by likely visibility impact, commercial importance, urgency and implementation effort.

  4. VALIDATE

    Check the live result.

    After release, we recrawl the affected pages and record what is fixed, what remains and what should happen next.
