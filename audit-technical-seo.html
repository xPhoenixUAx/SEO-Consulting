WHEN THIS WORK MATTERS

A technical audit is useful when the site is growing, changing or visibly underperforming — and especially when nobody can explain why.

  • Unexplained visibility loss
  • Redesign or platform migration
  • Large or complex inventories
  • Growth that has plateaued

MAKE THE SITE LEGIBLE BEFORE ASKING IT TO RANK.

A good audit does more than discover errors. It distinguishes a harmless imperfection from a structural problem that prevents important pages from being found, understood or consolidated correctly.

We connect technical evidence to commercial priority. Instead of handing over an unranked export, we show what is affected, why it matters, who should fix it and how the result will be validated.

THE SCOPE

FOUR SYSTEMS. ONE CLEAR PRIORITY MAP.

SEARCH ACCESS

WHAT WE INSPECT

Robots directives, XML sitemaps, status codes, canonical signals, duplicate URLs, index coverage and the routes search engines use to reach important pages.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

An evidence-backed issue map showing affected templates and URLs, the likely visibility cost and implementation notes that a developer can act on.

PAGE PURPOSE

WHAT WE INSPECT

Titles, headings, metadata, structured data, rendering, template rules and duplicated elements that blur the purpose of priority pages.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Template-level recommendations, priority-page examples and clear rules for creating distinct, readable signals at scale.

SITE STRUCTURE

WHAT WE INSPECT

Click depth, orphan pages, anchor context, faceted navigation and the pathways through which authority moves across the site.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A practical internal-linking map, hub recommendations and repeatable rules that reinforce the pages most important to the business.

QUALITY CONTROL

WHAT WE INSPECT

Mobile rendering, JavaScript dependencies, page experience, deployment risks and whether completed fixes behave as intended in the live environment.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Prioritized development tickets with acceptance criteria, followed by post-release checks so recommendations become verified changes rather than forgotten documents.

HOW THE WORK MOVES

FROM EVIDENCE TO A VERIFIED FIX.

  1. DISCOVER

    Establish the real site.

    We combine crawl data, search-platform evidence and business context so the diagnosis reflects the site users and search engines actually encounter.

  2. DIAGNOSE

    Find the cause, not the symptom.

    Patterns are traced across templates, sections and page types to reveal the smallest set of causes creating the greatest loss.

  3. PRIORITIZE

    Order work by consequence.

    Every recommendation is weighed against visibility impact, commercial value, urgency and realistic implementation effort.

  4. VALIDATE

    Confirm what changed.

    After release, we recrawl and check the relevant signals, documenting what is resolved and what should move into the next cycle.
