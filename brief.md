# SEO Consulting Website Brief

## Business

A premium SEO consulting company helping businesses become discoverable,
understood and trusted by search engines.

## Core idea

SEO should not be represented through dashboards, charts or generic marketing
cards.

The website should visualize search as a living system that discovers,
organizes and understands information.

## Hero experience

The hero begins as an almost empty black screen.

Small letters, words, URLs, queries and symbols gradually emerge from darkness.

They move organically in space and react to the cursor.

The cursor creates a subtle gravitational field.

The fragments gradually assemble into a large, sharp and readable headline:

BE
FOUND

The headline must remain typographically precise and immediately readable.
Particles should enrich and partially dissolve the edges of the letters,
not replace the typography completely.

When the user scrolls:

- the letters begin to break apart;
- particles move into depth;
- fragments transform into the next scene;
- the transition must feel continuous and cinematic.

## Visual character

- editorial;
- artistic;
- cinematic;
- premium;
- experimental;
- minimal;
- expressive typography;
- large negative space;
- controlled motion;
- black, warm white and one acid-lime accent.

## Avoid

- dashboards;
- analytics interfaces;
- cards;
- boxed sections;
- visible grids;
- generic SaaS design;
- blue gradients;
- glowing blobs;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- random floating UI;
- standard hero layouts;
- unclear particle-based typography;
- animation used only as decoration.

## Technical requirements

- Responsive from 1440px to 360px.
- Smooth performance.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Use semantic HTML.
- Avoid layout shifts.
- Keep the headline readable while effects animate around it.
